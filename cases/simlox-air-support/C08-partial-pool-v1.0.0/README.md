The child makes it two single-aircraft missions at 28 and 28.5 by changing `MissionType.NOS/MNOS` from 2/2 to 1/1 and adding one `OperationProfile` row. This creates crew-dependent preparation starts at 23.5 and 24 on a retained two-person P-CREW pool.
