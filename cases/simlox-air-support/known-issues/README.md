A01 observation-end probe classification: moving Control.SIMPE from 36 h to 7.5/9/9.9999/10/10.0001 h while keeping the scheduled 8-10 h mission caused conversion error #204 in every case and a 4-byte SXO. The printed error says missions may not return before the simulation period ends. Even the 10.0001 h variant was rejected; separate 10.5 h and 12 h controls passed. The threshold lies above 10.0001 h and at or below 10.5 h for this input, but the exact native margin/criterion remains unknown. This is an invalid input/capability boundary, not evidence of a J35 engine defect or a measured end-of-horizon event rule. Raw inputs, roundtrips and logs are indexed in observation-probes.json.
