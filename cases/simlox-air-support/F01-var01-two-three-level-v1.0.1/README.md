# F01.var01: two versus three physical levels with local reissue

This bounded integrated case joins the accepted [E05 two-level system→LRU repair/reissue](../E05-local-repair-reissue-v1.0.0/README.md) and [G01.var01 three-level system→LRU→SRU repair chain](../G01-var01-lru-sru-v1.0.1/README.md). It holds one aircraft at BASE-01, the same three missions, seed 1, 36-hour horizon, one local LRU spare and the system-level 10-minute LRU removal plus 20-minute fit in both arms. System and installed LRU traditional FRT are 1,200,000 per million operating hours; the three-level SRU FRT matches them. The SRU arm adds one local SRU spare, 0.75-hour LRU/SRU replacement overriding the general 1.5-hour LRU SURP, and one-hour failed SRU direct repair. The two-level arm instead repairs the removed LRU directly in one hour. The [frozen contract](contract.json), [two-level input](two-level-input.json), [three-level input](three-level-input.json) and [exact diffs](exact-diff.json) fix this comparison before native execution.

The first v1.0.0 nested diagnostic used the accepted E05 rate of 450,000, but one fixed-seed nested failure did not meet its predeclared repeated-reissue gate. Its 12 raw files and [NO-GO record](../F01-var01-nested-reissue-v1.0.0/diagnostic-2026-10-04.json) are preserved and excluded from acceptance. This v1.0.1 pair uses fresh inputs and run IDs. The two arms differ in six material/repair/stock/description tables. Adding a level changes more than one field and may alter random draws, so matching event times are not assumed.

Both guarded native arms completed import, export, calculation, JSON report and exact populated-table checks with converter errors 0 and logging warning #108. The two-level input preserved 20 populated tables; the three-level input preserved 21. Each arm has two installed LRU failures at h8.47072 and h20.8247, two aircraft-level LRU demands, and a repaired physical LRU that returns to stock before serving the second demand. Both early missions abort and the third succeeds; the aircraft is ready after repairs and at the terminal interval.

In the two-level arm, removed LRUs 1 and 2 each enter one-hour direct repair. Physical LRU1 returns to local stock h10.1081 and is issued for the second demand h21.3247. In the three-level arm, removed LRUs 2 and 4 reveal failed SRUs 3 and 5. Each LRU receives a local SRU spare and completes a 0.75-hour item-specific SURP. Failed SRUs undergo one-hour direct repair and return to SRU stock. Physical LRU2 returns to stock h9.85812 and is issued for the second demand h21.3247; repaired physical SRU3 also serves the second LRU repair. No backorder or resource shortage occurs.

The two arms' native ResultSummary and all 72 half-hour SystemResults and StockTotalResults rows are identical in this seed. They report two failures, two system repairs, two stock demands, no backorder, one of three missions successful and one ready aircraft at the end. The equal aggregate reports do not expose the extra SRU exchange; the physical event log and per-item stock identities do. Run `validate_pair.py` for the author read-only replay and `review/independent_replay.py` for the separate replay. Original SXI/SXO, event logs, reports, invocation and closure remain under ignored local `runs/` directories and are sealed by [the evidence index](evidence-index.json).

This accepts one bounded two/three-level contrast and two repeated local cycles. It does not establish ASSY behavior, more levels, extra repair stations, resource scarcity, PM, remote support, stochastic rates, GUI visual behavior, cross-engine equivalence or the general nested physical replacement issue #403. The broader F01 catalog and Issue #18 remain open.
