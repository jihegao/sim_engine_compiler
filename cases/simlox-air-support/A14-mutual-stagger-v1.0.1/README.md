# A14.var04/var05 staggered mutual and opposing dependency contrast (v1.0.1)

This bounded native CLI case compares one `MissionDependence` row with `MUTD=Y` against two opposing ordinary `MUTD=N` rows for two no-failure continuous missions staggered by 0.1 hour. It derives from the [accepted A05 continuous parent](../A05-continuous-baseline-v1.0.1/cli-scope-signoff-2026-09-30.md). SUPPORT is scheduled for 8–10, EXECUTE for 8.1–12.1, each with a separate aircraft. The [frozen contract](contract.json) and [exact diff](exact-diff.json) bind the four control, one-way, mutual and opposing inputs before four fresh native runs.

The installed manual describes MUTD=Y as mutual dependence and predicts deadlock for two opposing ordinary dependencies. Two earlier [same-time](../A14-continuous-mutual-v1.0.0/README.md) and [staggered](../A14-mutual-stagger-v1.0.0/README.md) diagnostics contradicted their frozen deadlock predictions and remain NO-GO. The v1.0.1 contract explicitly predicted the *observed* narrow equivalence for fresh runs; it does not turn those failed diagnostics into accepted runs.

Control SUPPORT and EXECUTE succeed at 10 and 12.1. One-way dependence aborts EXECUTE at 10 when SUPPORT succeeds. Both MUTD=Y and two opposing ordinary rows suspend SUPPORT at 8, resume it at 8.1, start both tasks at 8.1 and abort EXECUTE at 10. The mutual and opposing native event logs are byte-identical, and all native report tables apart from `ProblemDescription` match. All 32 half-hour System and Stock bins reconcile with 6, 3.9, 3.8 and 3.8 operating aircraft-hours, respectively, no failures, repairs or stock demand, and two ready aircraft at hour 16. The [author replay](review/author-validation-2026-10-01.json) passes 970 checks; a [separate read-only replay](review/independent-readonly-2026-10-01.json) passes 7,371 checks.

The [sign-off](cli-scope-signoff-2026-10-01.md) records immutable run IDs and raw SHA-256 hashes. Bulky native artifacts remain in ignored local `runs/`. To reproduce on the licensed host, run `build.py` and `describe_diff.py`, then invoke `run_native_ssh.ps1` through authenticated session-0 SSH once per variant with fresh RunIds, and run `validate.py`. Original IDs cannot be reused. This case establishes equality only for this one stagger, unit and no-failure configuration; it does not establish general MUTD equivalence or refute every manual deadlock scenario. Other schedules, readiness constraints, DTTOL, GUI visual content and cross-engine behavior remain unverified. Issue #18 stays open.
