# Capability index

The [progressive catalog v2](progressive-catalog-v2.md) and [machine catalog](catalog-v2.json) are frozen planning snapshots: 108 cases, 579 named variants and 133 mapped input tables do not mean those scopes are published. The [publication index](publication-index-v1.json) lists **41 accepted bounded CLI packages covering 26 distinct case IDs** as of 2026-09-30. Each linked sign-off or evidence report defines the verified behavior and limits. Full manual-method coverage, GUI-specific behavior and cross-engine equivalence require their own evidence. The [manual coverage](manual-coverage-v2.json) and [findings](known-issues/manual-review-v2.md) retain the broader unverified methods and source conflicts.
