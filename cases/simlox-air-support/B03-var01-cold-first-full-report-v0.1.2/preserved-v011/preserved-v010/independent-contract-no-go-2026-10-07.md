**Blocking contradiction:** `full_report_gate.system_integrals_h.ready` declares **29⅙ aircraft-hours**. The source timeline yields `SystemResults.NSYRY` ready and unassigned for h0–7, h10–11, h14–15 and h18–36: exactly **27 hours**. The sealed accepted 72-row report independently integrates `NSYRY` to 27.0. The difference, **2⅙ hours**, is the prepared and assigned prelaunch waiting state `NSYMR`: h7.5–8 and h11⅙–12 and h15⅙–16. `NSYMP` preparation integrates to ⅚ hour, `NSYMO` mission operation to 6 hours, `NSYMA` assigned to 9 hours, and `NSYA` available to 36 hours. The contract's 29⅙ value comes from subtracting only preparation and mission operation from the horizon and thus merges `NSYRY` with `NSYMR`. A source-derived 4,044-cell oracle enforcing that integral would reject the accepted report, while redefining “ready” after implementation would weaken the frozen gate.

The manual sections defining SystemResults states (physical pages 629 onward) are needed in the corrected report-field derivation; the current contract lists source/task preparation pages but not these report semantics. A new version should retain v0.1.0 and this NO_GO as excluded history, specify each of `NSYRY`, `NSYMR`, `NSYMP`, `NSYMO`, `NSYMA` and `NSYA` integrals by field, and keep the exact 4,044-cell, null, zero, integer and printed-time bounds. The no-stock `StockTotalResults` presentation, including `PNB` baseline and undefined waiting ratios, should be expressly bounded to the accepted source/manual semantics. The already published B03 v1.0.2 physical behavior remains accepted; this rejection only blocks the new full-report retrospective classification contract.
