# G09.var01 mission-selected RBD

This case gives one aircraft two mission types with different reliability blocks. It derives from the accepted [G04 series/parallel controls](../G04-series-active-parallel-v1.0.1/cli-scope-signoff-2026-10-02.md), retaining their physical POS-A/POS-B failure modes, half-hour direct CM, scheduled h8/h20/h32 two-hour missions, seed, replication and horizon. `BASIC-MISSION` at h8 and h32 selects a new two-position series block; `PAR-MISSION` at h20 selects the accepted active one-of-two parallel block. The two mission types have identical duration and other behavior fields. The [contract](contract.json) and [exact diff](exact-diff.json) pin the eight changed or added business tables.

The mixed native run has the same B/B/A failure times as the accepted G04 controls: h8.62763, h20.8222 and h33.0996. The first POS-B failure is critical and aborts BASIC-MISSION; the second POS-B failure is noncritical during PAR-MISSION, which succeeds at h22; the third POS-A failure is critical and aborts BASIC-MISSION. Accepted all-series and all-parallel controls classify these same events critical and noncritical respectively. The mixed ResultSummary has mission success fraction 1/3, and 72 System/Stock bins reconcile three failures, three repairs, no stock movement and terminal ready1. Identical event times are observed in this seeded comparison; they do not prove a general common random stream or population result.

The guarded session-0 SSH run had zero converter errors/notes and reviewed warning #108, exact 25-populated-table native roundtrip, and 13 locally sealed raw files. [Author replay](author-validation.json) passes 94/94; [independent replay](review/independent-validation-2026-10-03.json) verifies the result separately through the [evidence index](evidence-index.json).

This establishes one mission type-to-mode-to-RBD selection pattern for one aircraft.
