# H02.var03 damaged-LRU repairability endpoints

This pair takes the accepted [H02.var01 primary-LRU source](../H02-var01-primary-lru-damage-v1.0.0/README.md) byte for byte as the repairable (`REPF=1`) arm. The discard arm changes only `DamageItem.REPF` to zero and the problem description. Both retain one directly mounted LRU, one local spare, certain in-flight BATTLE-HIT damage, explicit damage replacement, ordinary one-hour item repair data, three fixed missions, seed1 and horizon36. The [pre-run contract](contract.json) and [exact diff](exact-diff.json) freeze that comparison.

Two serial guarded session-0 SSH runs exactly roundtrip all 24 authored row-containing tables, convert with zero errors and preserve GUI PID13340. In the `REPF=1` arm, physical LRU IDs 1/2/3 are damaged and removed; spare IDs 0/1/2 are issued; each removed LRU receives a one-hour direct item repair and returns to stock. All three missions launch and abort on damage, and the aircraft ends ready with nominal stock one.

In the `REPF=0` arm, LRU1 is damaged during the h8 mission, spare LRU0 is issued at h9.28145, and LRU1 is discarded at h9.44811. The aircraft becomes ready at h9.78145 and launches the h20 mission. LRU2 is damaged at h20.1965; the replacement demand backorders at h20.393; LRU2 is removed and discarded at h20.5596. No item direct repair or stock return occurs. The h32 mission cancels, and the aircraft ends unready with one backorder and nominal stock zero.

Each arm has 72 aligned half-hour System and Stock bins and one ResultSummary row. Damage markers are 3 versus 2; completed system repair markers are 3 versus 1; stock demand markers are 3 versus 2. The discard arm has terminal `NSYRY=0`, `NSYUA=1`, `NBO=1`, `PNB=0` and `MSTF=2/3`. `PNB` is a nominal report measure; physical issue, discard and absence of return are established from the item-identified model log. The [sign-off](cli-scope-signoff-2026-10-02.md) and [evidence index](evidence-index.json) bind the source, native and review artifacts. Local raw runs remain ignored by Git and are indexed by SHA-256.

This is one seed and one primary-LRU endpoint pair. It does not establish an intermediate repair probability, other damaged item structures or a general replacement fallback rule. The explicit DamageReplacement and ordinary ItemRepair data are present in both arms; the contrast isolates the `REPF` endpoint in this configuration.
