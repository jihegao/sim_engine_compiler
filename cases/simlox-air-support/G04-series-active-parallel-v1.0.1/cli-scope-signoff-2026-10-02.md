## Behavior, review and limits

Both runs draw the same failures: FM-POS-B at hours 8.62763 and 20.8222, then FM-POS-A at 33.0996. The series log calls each critical and aborts all three missions; its event log is byte-identical to the accepted G02 parent and its non-description report tables equal the parent's. In active parallel, each isolated failed branch is noncritical while the other remains functional; all three missions succeed at hours 10, 22 and 34. With `ONLM=N`, direct local CM begins after mission end and lasts 0.5 hour in each arm. Both report `NFAIL=3` and `NREPT=3`: `NFAIL` counts observed failure modes even when the active parallel system keeps working. Series mission success fraction is 0 and integrated operating time is about 4.049385 aircraft-hours; active parallel values are 1 and 6 hours. The Stock ledger has zero demand and backorder throughout; both arms end with one ready aircraft and no open CM.

An excluded v1.0.0 series diagnostic imported and exported, but native conversion rejected missing leaf modes with two error #464 records and a four-byte unusable SXO. Its exact failed input and closure/log fingerprints are retained in `diagnostic-predecessor.json`; eight diagnostic raw seals remain local and are excluded from the accepted 24. Version 1.0.1 adds the manual's explicit leaf-mode mapping; it did not promote the failed run.

The author source-bound replay passed **47/47** checks with no differences (report SHA-256 `0d74930db0c40504542e172a38a7b27324dae3bc520991a01d4f7a361c89308f`).
