# D10.var02: three counters for one preventive maintenance task

This bounded CLI case adds `MaterielPMInterval` rows `OPHOURS=3` and `CALHOURS=24` to the frozen [D10.var01 mission-count parent](../D10-var01-missions-v1.0.0/README.md), retaining `MaterielPM.MISSIONS=2` and the same five fixed missions. Native PM work occurs at 12–13 after the four-hour first mission, 21–22 after two later one-hour missions, 46–47 after a no-flight gap, and 53–54 after two further one-hour missions. These separated times support inference that the operation-hour, mission-count, calendar, and mission-count thresholds trigger in that order and reset the competing counters. The engine log identifies PM work, not the winning counter directly.

The report's `NPMS` marker for the first PM appears in the 11–11.5-hour bin, when `OPHOURS=3` is reached during the mission; PM work starts at return hour 12 in the individual event log. At hour 47 the next mission briefly waits while the calendar PM completes, then allocates the aircraft and launches at 48. The paired [mission-only control](../D10-var01-missions-v1.0.0/README.md) has two PMs instead of four.
