# F02 paired integrated teaching fleet, v1.0.2

This bounded pair combines the accepted A04 two-day two-aircraft profile, B04 cross-midnight one-hour turnround, C03 one-worker preparation crew, D05 calendar PM and shared maintainer, E04 two-hour FAST base/depot transport, and F01 traditional aircraft/LRU/SRU physical chain. The 60-hour, one-seed inputs differ only in `RISK-MISSION.MFF`: zero in the deterministic arm, one in the risk arm. [The pre-run contract](contract.json), [exact D05 and paired diff](exact-diff.json), and [builder](build.py) freeze the model before native runs.

The earlier [v1.0.0 diagnostic](../F02-var01-var02-integrated-fleet-v1.0.0/diagnostic.json) showed that local nested LRU repair won the base backorder before any depot transfer. [V1.0.1](../F02-var01-var02-integrated-fleet-v1.0.1/diagnostic.json) extended local repair to four hours but still generated no depot demand. Both pairs are excluded from behavioral publication. This version places LRU/SRU repair, SRU stock and nested replacement at `DEPOT-01`, while aircraft replacement stays at `BASE-01`; it restores F01's 0.75-hour nested replacement duration.

The guarded session-0 SSH native runs are `f02-pair-v102-det-ssh-20261004-01` and `f02-pair-v102-risk-ssh-20261004-01`. Both completed import, export, calculation, report and authored-table roundtrip under the host-wide native mutex with GUI PID 13340 preserved. Conversion was Errors 0, Warnings 1 (known #108), Notes 0. Each run has 12 locally sealed raw files and 120 half-hour System, Stock and Resource result bins.

The deterministic arm has four successful two-aircraft missions, four completed calendar PMs and no random fault. The risk arm has one critical aircraft fault at hour 46.4896, two mission successes, one abort and one later cancellation. Depot LRU 2 reaches the base in exactly two hours to clear its backorder; failed LRU 4 travels upstream for an SRU 5 replacement, and both repaired LRU 4 and SRU 5 return to depot stock. Four PMs complete in each arm; both aircraft are ready at hour 60, no stock backorder or resource wait remains. The [author replay](validate_native.py) passes 463/463 checks, including every resource utilization bin and each risk-arm backorder bin. Independent review is recorded separately in `review/`.

Scope is one fixed seed, two aircraft, one base/depot pair, one LRU/SRU chain, one preparation crew and one maintenance worker. The pair establishes these combined native CLI paths and ledgers for the first small teaching fleet. It does not claim generic stochastic coverage, expanded scale, GUI visual parity or cross-engine equivalence. F02 and Issue #18 remain open for subsequent variants and dependent cases.
