Two aircraft launch separate h8 missions; two high-rate LRU failures produce two distinct same-item spare demands. Base opens with zero spare and depot with two. The [frozen contract](contract.json) and [exact diff](exact-diff.json) pin both arms; their only difference is the basic ONDEM profile's `MINBS=1` versus `MINBS=2` and description.

The first local backorder therefore lasts 2.24992 instead of 2 hours. The two upward failed-item repairs, two mission aborts and terminal ready aircraft remain reconciled. System/Stock report differences are confined to bins 16, 20 and 21; the ResultSummary matches.

This demonstrates a two-demand threshold for the **same item type on one general link**. It does not establish mixed-item/system batching, shared-data equivalence across different transport tables, end-of-horizon partial batches, larger thresholds, GUI visuals or cross-engine parity.
