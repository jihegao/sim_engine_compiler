In the three accepted inputs, its value is byte-identical; **only** the P-CREW `ResourceAllocation.RQTY` cell changes across 2/1/0. Require this sole positional cell delta, preserving every other cell including the description. This prevents a later checker from accepting a broader source change than the reused evidence has.
2. The report gate names 72-bin System and Resource results, but the accepted default report has **six** tables: `ProblemDescription` (one row), `ResultSummary` (one), `SystemResults` (72), `StockTotalResults` (72), `ResourceResults` (72), and `ResourceResultsTotal` (one). The phrase “complete report ledgers” is not an exact acceptance gate. Require all six table schemas/row counts and all applicable cell-level reconciliation or pair comparisons, including StockTotal, Summary, ResourceResultsTotal and description, before publication. Keep the h7.58333 cancellation an observation rather than a general deadline rule.
