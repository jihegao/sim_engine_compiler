## Failed controls, review and limits

The preserved [v1.0.0 probe](../A18-alternate-unit-transfer-v1.0.0/README.md) **failed its transfer contract** because the alternate unit had no explicit profile: SIMLOX generated a 0–36 default mission that occupied both alternate aircraft. Its guarded native closure SHA-256 is `c2d348cb7f7c977bc26f6780366a8eaa8612825127ee867b9a8872df96cda24a`. The preserved [v1.0.1 pair](../A18-alternate-unit-transfer-v1.0.1/README.md) suppressed that default mission, but the `ENALU=N` run emitted warning #188 (“Alternate units are disabled. Data ignored.”) **and still transferred** exactly as `ENALU=Y`: their event logs are byte-identical. That frozen switch contrast is **NO-GO**, and no broad `ENALU` rule is inferred. These are diagnostics, not published variants.

[Author read-only replay](validate.py), SHA-256 `3db0673c58d04945f33e7632a22e17f215f6e0f4fdc6b01abb4a77befef0296f`, passed 852 checks; its [result](review/author-validation-2026-10-01.json) SHA-256 is `d6ca326b19a54c0ffbb2e9b61e726aaf578e8714ddf5dd9ab016b812a631894e`. A separate [independent raw reviewer](review/independent_readonly.py), SHA-256 `55894db89a33fe7566ae4e8a7ab5d4ad6857376b597a551e9cec873827bdb8fa`, imported no author implementation and made no native calls.
