# F03.var03 two-base risk-demand location comparison

This bounded pair derives from the accepted F03.var02 depot-only risk fleet. The control has its two-aircraft risk unit at BASE-01 and single-aircraft teaching unit at BASE-02. The alternate swaps their `Unit.STID` values and the corresponding `MissionPreparation.STID` and `MissionTurnround.STID` rows. The [exact diff](exact-diff.json) records eight station cells and one descriptive cell. Both bases retain equal crew and maintainer capacity, zero opening LRU stock, matching system repair and PM definitions, and two-hour depot legs. Aircraft deployment counts, mission schedules, seed and 60-hour horizon remain fixed.

Both native arms have the same risk event times and same three LRU demands at hours 23.74 and twice 48.2231. In the control, demand, three two-hour backorders, depot transport and affected aircraft repairs occur at BASE-01. In the alternate, they occur at BASE-02. The one-aircraft BASE2 mission succeeds h8–10 at the opposite base in each arm. SIMLOX renumbers aircraft when unit locations swap, so aircraft identities are matched by unit and event sequence rather than by numeric ID. All aggregate report tables except `ProblemDescription` are equal under these symmetric configurations. That aggregate equality does not identify the station of demand; typed events do.

## Reproduce

The [contract](contract.json) pins the accepted parent input, closure and signoff, both frozen inputs, exact diff and acceptance limits. `python build.py` and `python describe_diff.py` verify the frozen bytes. Run `run_native_ssh.ps1 -Arm risk_base1|risk_base2 -RunId <new-id>` serially under session-0 SSH. The runner verifies its `sshd.exe` ancestor, `Global\SimloxIssue18NativeQueue` mutex, active GUI PID 13340/session 1, source hashes and installed executable, then imports, exports, calculates, reports, checks every populated authored table and seals local raw files. `python validate_native.py` replays accepted run IDs; an independent reviewer uses [separate code](review/independent_replay.py). The [evidence index](evidence-index.json) lists SHA-256 seals. Raw SXI/SXO, logs and reports remain ignored locally.

This single seed shows location-sensitive item demand with symmetric support settings. It does not establish unequal route times, capacity or stock, transport contention, lateral supply, autonomous stations, interbase aircraft transfer, larger networks, GUI visual behavior or cross-engine equivalence.
