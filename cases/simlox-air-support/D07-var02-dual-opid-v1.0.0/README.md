# D07.var02 traditional OPHOURS and KM failure rates, v1.0.0

This bounded case derives from accepted D02.var01 and adds one custom KM operation parameter, 60 KM per mission hour, and equal 5000 per million KM traditional failure rates to the system and its installed LRU. Base OPHOURS rates remain 300000 per million operating hours. The only parent changes are the description, the custom operation parameter row, and the three additional tables `MissionTypeUtilization`, `ItemFailureRate`, and `SystemFailureRate`. The [contract](contract.json) fixed the hypothesis and gates before native execution; the [exact diff](parent-diff.json) is reproducible.

The installed 2017 manual, PDF pages 183–184, says failure contributions across operation parameters add and optional failure-rate tables override same-key base values. Its pages 257–258 disallow `MISSIONS` and `CALHOURS` in `SystemFailureRate` and require child rates not to exceed the mother rates. D02.var06 confirmed that the installed executable rejects `MISSIONS` and `CALHOURS` in traditional item rates. Accepted A15 established the KM utilization row and the specialized SOACC report setting. This case tests custom KM plus OPHOURS; it does not claim a statistical measurement of the sum from one seed.
