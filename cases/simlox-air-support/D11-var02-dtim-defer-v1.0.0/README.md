# D11.var02: two-mission PM deferment window

This bounded CLI case changes only `MaterielPM.DTIM` from zero to 0.3, plus a descriptive label, in the [six-request control](../D11-var02-dtim-control-v1.0.0/README.md). With `PMINT=4`, the installed SIMLOX manual gives `ceil(0.3 × 4)=2` extra missions before PM becomes critical. At the fourth return, hour 25, native output marks `MS4` noncritical and creates its PM task, then pauses it for the fifth and sixth missions. At the fifth return, 26.25, PM remains noncritical. At the sixth return, 27.5, it becomes critical; the same task resumes and performs its one-hour subtask from 27.5–28.5. All six missions succeed.
