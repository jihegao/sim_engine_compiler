This bounded case derives from the accepted [A02 repeated-mission input](../A02-single-aircraft-repeated-missions/cli-scope-signoff-2026-09-29.md). The [builder](build.py) and [exact diff](exact-diff.json) bind the source to accepted A02 input SHA-256 `84c5cca6c06384b850d2420297a3f5c2f5c553904f123f690190fdb42475e0b8`.

The guarded native run `a07-regenerative-relief-v100-ssh-20260930-01` creates four sub-missions. Aircraft #0 departs at 8 and 12; aircraft #1 departs at 10 and 14. The first three on-station intervals meet at hours 11 and 13 and cover hours 9–15 without a gap. The final child reaches the task area at hour 15 and immediately starts return, so it has zero on-station time, then completes at the regeneration period end, hour 16. All four children report mission success. The 72 half-hour bins integrate 14 aircraft mission-operation hours, with zero failures, repairs, preparation exposure or inventory demand; both aircraft are ready at the hour-36 report end.
