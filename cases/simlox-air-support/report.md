# A01/A02 case-library slice: native CLI evidence and limits

This is issue #18's first two teaching cases on main `595cb5eee9987324b19a8c3566f201f937e05f15`. The vehicle is a generic one-aircraft asset, not an F35 or a J35-equivalent model. PR #15 and older phase evidence were read only. The existing Python/J35 contract and Rust engine were not changed.

## Implemented and reproduced

The 26-entry [catalog](catalog.json) names A01/A02 as the only implemented cases; the other 24 stay planned. Each implemented case has a predeclared [contract](A01-single-aircraft-single-mission/contract.json), parameters, SIMLOX table and GUI mapping, checked native JSON, immutable-run manifest, result summary, acceptance state and raw-evidence index. The row-free [native table schema](native-schema.json) came from the phase6 `b-zero-stock-repair.json` structure (source SHA-256 `1b01a20111aed6c6fc96339c512147c862178967c01e67421eb380379b45f8d0`), without copying model rows or introducing a Project JSON interpreter.

The final A01 native input SHA-256 is `8bbd31d05630cc4a44c4b03b715d17e2f4a1ee1601352952089efd3e584f98b7`; A02 is `84c5cca6c06384b850d2420297a3f5c2f5c553904f123f690190fdb42475e0b8`. The [parent diff](A02-single-aircraft-repeated-missions/parent-diff.json) SHA-256 is `61957e4ce0c33bd1b364757b30373d12221d1186734a9556e920d5136168f583`: exactly two additional `OperationProfile` rows at 20 h and 32 h, no other business-table change. These are explicit scheduled rows, not SIMLOX cyclic generation. Both use one `TEACH-AIR-01` SID with `QTYPS=1`, a 2 h mission, 1 h notice lead, 20 min `DTIM`, one base, no items, PM, CM, stock, preparation business row or constrained resources, and a 36 h normal result window.

Native SIMLOX 2017 executable SHA-256 `fe24c5bffc0c0641894bb335484cfb83993e3db2bd60122ddadfedd39a8c4db0` ran `jimport`, `jexport`, `calc` and `jsonreport` sequentially with single-thread calc. Final and independent replay runs used fresh immutable directories and each passed zero conversion errors, nonempty business-row roundtrip, nonempty SXI/SXO, parsed report and ENLOG, hand timeline and state assertions. Their [A01](A01-single-aircraft-single-mission/evidence-index.json) and [A02](A02-single-aircraft-repeated-missions/evidence-index.json) indexes give byte hashes and absolute paths for every raw artifact. The coordinator independently regenerated both JSON inputs into a new directory and obtained the checked byte hashes before replay. Native execution was through the previously successful coordinator SSH PowerShell process context, not through a competing local runner.

The same stable SID appears in all A02 start/completion and ready-transition records. Each launch has one completion, the aircraft becomes unavailable at notice, returns ready at completion, and the three mission intervals do not overlap. `MSTF/MSUF=1` are native aggregate fractions. Wave and sortie counts here come from the first-repetition ENLOG and checked scheduled rows; with one aircraft per wave they happen to be numerically equal, and this slice does not validate multi-aircraft accounting.

The installed table dictionary `UserDoc/Table relations/DatabaseDictionary_SIMLOX.txt` defines `MissionType.DURN` in hours (line 452), `MSUCPT` as a fraction (454), `TFOUT` as mission out-time fraction (455) and `TFRET` as return-time fraction (456). This input explicitly sets `DURN=2`, `MSUCPT=1`, `TFOUT=TFRET=.25`. A01's ENLOG places mission launch/completion at 8/10 h and the inner mission task at 8.5/9.5 h, consistent with those fractions; `MSUCPT=1` coincides with completion in this no-fault case. `OPHOURS` is a built-in operation parameter identifier accepted on `System.OPID`; no row in `OperationParameter` is needed. Blank `ControlParameters`/`GlobalParameters`/`OperationParameter` instances have no nonempty business row and were imported/exported unchanged. No unsupported default is inferred from nulls.

The initial diagnostic model included a zero-duration `MissionPreparation` row. Native ENLOG then showed same-time preparation start/end and `SystemResults.NPSTA=1` (A01) or 3 (A02), so it failed the declared no-preparation contract. The final model omits that row: same-time native lifecycle text remains, but `NPSTA=0`, there is no task-resource work, and behavior passes. Initial raw outputs are retained under the ignored v1 evidence tree, indexed in [diagnostic evidence](diagnostic-evidence.json), and are not labeled accepted. Warnings `#108` (ENLOG enabled) and `#126` (this intentionally item-free system) are listed and explained by the validator; any other warning is rejected. SIMLOX's raw JSON report contains a trailing comma; original bytes remain untouched and a separately hashed normalized copy is parsed.

## Observation-window investigation

The normal 36 h run finishes all missions. It does not establish what SIMLOX does if a mission crosses the simulation period or the result window. The first A01 matrix changed only `Control.SIMPE/RCEND` and `ResultCollection.RCEND` to 7.5, 9, 9.9999, 10 and 10.0001 h. All five models imported and exported exact nonempty business rows, but calc reported error `#204` (some missions may not return before simulation period ends), wrote a 4-byte SXO and produced no report. This proves those inputs are invalid under the native converter; it does not prove a cancellation or end-priority rule. The raw invalid SXI/SXO/logs and input hashes are retained under `data/local/simlox-case-library/v1/horizon-runs/` and `horizon-inputs/`. Two positive controls at 10.5 and 12 h ran with zero conversion errors and a normal 8-10 h completion, bounding this input's native feasibility threshold above 10.0001 h and at or below 10.5 h. A formula involving `DTIM` remains a hypothesis.

A second matrix keeps `SIMPE=36` and varies only `Control.RCEND` and `ResultCollection.RCEND`. All five variants ran with zero conversion errors and full raw output. The ENLOG still covers the 8–10 h mission, while `SystemResults` stops at the requested 7.5/9/9.9999/10/10.0001 h collection boundary. Its `FTMO` integral tracks 0/1/1.999899864/2/2 h; an independent event-clipped calculation gives 0/1/1.9999/2/2 h. `ResultSummary.RTIUP` remains 36 h, and `MSTF/MSUF` are 0/0 before launch and 1/1 at all later collection ends, including 9 h before completion. These are different output boundaries; no native simulation-stop rule was established. See the [observation probe index](observation-probes.md) for per-variant hashes, event lines and precision.

## Acceptance boundary

Machine-readable [A01](A01-single-aircraft-single-mission/acceptance.json) and [A02](A02-single-aircraft-repeated-missions/acceptance.json) states separate CLI model validity, CLI behavior, independent replay, GUI calibration/review, publication and cross-engine pairing. CLI normal-path behavior is verified. GUI access still awaits a private handoff; GUI-first ordering did not occur, and neither case has unqualified reusable publication acceptance. A02 is only a provisional CLI derivation of A01. The minimal [AMD128 pairing contract](pairing-contract.json) states units, identities, events, counts, observation and raw fingerprints, but AMD128 pairing is unverified. A01/A02 cannot resolve J35 wave-20 cancellation or the 403 nested-LRU physical replacement boundary.
