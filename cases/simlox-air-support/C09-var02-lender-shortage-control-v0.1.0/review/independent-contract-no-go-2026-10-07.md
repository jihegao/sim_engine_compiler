## Blocking defects

1. `case_id` is `C09.var02.lender-shortage-control.retrospective`. The accepted C09.var04 and C09.var03 contracts and all three prospective C09.var02 versions use the canonical catalog case ID `C09`, with `variant_id` holding the specific arm. This candidate already has `variant_id=C09.var02`. A separate descriptive classification identifier can retain the longer name, but the case identity must not fork the catalog lineage.
2. The precision rule leaves all other genuinely fractional event-derived report fields at “separate field-specific bounds from actual printed-time precision” without a numerical formula or a field/edge classification. That leaves the required 1,754-cell pass condition underdetermined until checker implementation. A new contract should predeclare, for each event-derived field class, the exposure weights and denominator (0.5-hour bin for occupancy, 12-hour horizon for ResourceResultsTotal means, source mission DURN for MTMF/STMF, and **no bin-width division** for elapsed-hour SYDT) and calculate the absolute bound from each raw printed edge's decimal resolution (`0.5×10^-d` hour for `d` printed decimals), with an explicit numeric serialization allowance. Scheduled source-exact whole-bin and integer/count/null/ID values remain exact. The two [9,9.5) calibrated cells retain only their named ≤3e-6 exception, and interior FTMO retains ≤5e-7; neither may be absorbed into a broad default bound. Rejection probes should cross the declared bound for each class.

These are contract defects, independent of whether a later checker could be made to pass this one observed report. Preserve v0.1.0 as excluded. A v0.1.1 contract with canonical `case_id`, explicit fractional-bound formula/table, fresh classification identity, and literal v0.1.0 NO_GO pin can be reviewed before any implementation. The original v1.0.2 prospective failure and unrun shortage arm must remain unchanged.
