# F03.var01 two-base crew-capacity comparison

This bounded case derives from the accepted F02.var01 deterministic two-aircraft fleet. It retains the BASE-01 two-aircraft missions, depot LRU/SRU chain, base crew and maintainer, CAL24 PM and 60-hour window. The [builder](build.py) adds an A17-shaped second base and unit with one more `TEACH-AIR-01`, its own single-aircraft mission, preparation, turnround, maintainer and crew, and a two-hour depot route. Both arms have identical physical and operational inputs except `ResourceAllocation(AIR_BASIC, P-CREW, BASE-02).RQTY`: staffed 1, unstaffed 0. Descriptive text also differs. The [exact diff](exact-diff.json) records all 14 parent tables with appended rows and this sole paired business change.

The staffed run's BASE-02 aircraft ID 2 receives the crew at hour 7, prepares for 0.5 hour, launches at 8, succeeds at 10 and returns ready at 11. The unstaffed run requests that crew at 7, waits without work, cancels at 8.5, and returns the aircraft ready without launching. At BASE-01, aircraft IDs 0 and 1 start and succeed on four two-aircraft waves at the same times in both arms. All three aircraft each complete two CAL24 PMs. There is no item fault, repair demand or backorder in either arm.

The pair changes `ResultSummary.MTMF` from 1 to 0.8 (five demands, four successes when unstaffed) and `STMF` from 1 to about 0.888889 (eight of nine planned aircraft sorties). The 120 half-hour System bins conserve three aircraft and integrate 18 versus 16 operating aircraft-hours, with six PM aircraft-hours in both. Physical resource grants and failed requests explain aggregate `ResourceResults`: four versus three available resources, 10.5 versus 10 booked resource-hours, and 3 versus 4.5 queued resource-hours. The extra 1.5 queue hours in the unstaffed arm are the BASE-02 preparation wait from hour 7 to cancellation at 8.5. At hour 60, three aircraft are ready and no worker request or stock backorder remains in either arm.
